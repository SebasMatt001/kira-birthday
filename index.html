Chapter one · The beginning

Somehow, my raid leader became my favorite person?

Of all the ways we could have possibly met, we started started in World of Warcraft. You were my raid leader, and neither of us could have known that a game would introduce me to someone who would become so important to me.

Somewhere between you asking me if I will make it to raids, playing games, conversations, and all the little moments in between, you became much more than someone I played games with. You became someone I look forward to talking to, laughing with, teasing, ragebaiting and spending all of my time with.

Now we have our own little collection of adventures: World of Warcraft, League of Legends, TFT, Valheim, and whatever other game we decide to get ourselves into next. Even when we're on opposite sides of the country, those moments make me feel like we are side by side and nothing in the world can bother me.

Valheim is where we really started talking and hanging out. We both gave our characters the last name Erikson without knowing the other one had; it was just meant to be hehe

Florida and California might be far apart, but I'm really glad that a certain raid leader makes all those miles worth it.

The final chapter · Just for you

Happy birthday, mi amor. 💜

I wish I could be there with you today. I wish I could celebrate with you in person, give you a hug, kiss you, see your smile, and spend the whole day making sure you know how special you are to me and doing whatever it is you wanna do.

Even though we're far apart, I hope you feel how much I love you and also all of your friends and family. I hope this next year right before the big 30(!) brings you happiness, exciting adventures, good music, delicious milk tea, and lots of little moments that make you smile.

I'm grateful that we met, even if it happened in the most nerd way it could have actually happened. I wouldn't have guessed that meeting my raid leader would lead to someone I love and care about this much. I'm so happy that you are part of my life.

Thank you for being who you are. Thank you for sharing your time, your laughter, your music, your games, and pieces of your life with me. I hope I can keep making memories with you for the rest of our lives.

No matter how many miles are between Florida and California, you're the love of my life, And today, I hope this little corner of the internet reminds you of that.

This website isn't much compared to what I wish I could give you in person, but I made it for you, and every little part of it is meant to make you smile. I know that I will see you soon.

Happy birthday. I love you. ♥
